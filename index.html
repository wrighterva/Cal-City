Season Rules

  1. Each team plays every other team once.
  2. Best record normally receives the championship bye.
  3. If tied for best record, the defending champion gets the bye when involved.
  4. If all three tie, the previous champion gets the bye.
  5. The other two teams play the semifinal.
  6. Semifinal winner plays the bye team for the championship.
  7. Champion receives $100M; runner-up receives $50M.
