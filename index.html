🏀 Calvin's City Basketball

3 Teams3 Players Each4 Quarters

Each team has a C, SG, and PG. Every player gets two point rolls and two rebound rolls each quarter.

Season Format

  1. Each team plays every other team once.
  2. Best record normally receives the championship bye.
  3. If teams tie for the best record, the defending champion gets the bye when involved.
  4. If all three teams tie, the previous champion gets the bye.
  5. The other two teams play the semifinal.
  6. Semifinal winner plays the bye team for the championship.
