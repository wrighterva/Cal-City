ℹ️ Basketball Information

Season Rules

  1. Each team plays every other team once.
  2. Best record normally receives the championship bye.
  3. If tied for best record, the defending champion gets the bye when involved.
  4. If all three tie, the previous champion gets the bye.
  5. Season 29 starts from the permanent Season 28 championship record; Season 28 champion Dunks enters as defending champion.
  6. The other two teams play the semifinal.
  7. Semifinal winner plays the bye team for the championship.
  8. The team receiving the playoff bye gets $60M.
  9. Champion receives $100M; runner-up receives $50M.

Game Rules

  • Each player gets two point dice and two rebound dice per quarter.
  • There are four quarters.
  • Overtime uses one point die and one rebound die per player until the tie is broken.

Ticket Revenue

Every game gets two dice. Each die is worth $10M. Total range: $20M–$120M.

Ticket revenue is split 70% to the winner and 30% to the loser.

Shoe Revenue

Each shoe company gets two dice rolls per game ($20M–$120M) when it has an active player deal. TV Company also rolls two dice ($20M–$120M) and receives an additional 10% of total shoe-company revenue. The TV share does not reduce shoe-company revenue.

Sports Bets Company Debt

Sports Bets Company can issue debt to raise cash. Citizens and businesses may buy portions of an issuance; basketball teams and Sports Bets Company itself cannot buy. At payoff, each holder receives principal plus a one-time 0.25% interest payment on the principal they purchased. The debt register tracks each owner, ownership percentage, principal, interest and payout.
